## About Me

While studying economics, I developed an interest in econometrics and the use of mathematical models to understand real-world behavior. Since then, I've gained over five years of experience in data analytics, from reporting and performance measurement to using data science and modeling techniques to uncover insights that inform decision-making.

Outside of work, I'm curious about the world and enjoy challenging myself. Traveling has given me the opportunity to learn Spanish and Portuguese, while running brings structure and enjoyment to my life and gives me goals to work toward. I've completed three full marathons, and one of my proudest personal achievements is the personal record I set in my most recent race.
